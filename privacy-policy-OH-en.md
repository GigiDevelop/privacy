# Privacy Policy — OmniHealth

**Last updated:** June 2026  
**App:** OmniHealth (`com.gigidevelop.omnihealth`)  
**Contact:** develop@abcdef.it

---

## 1. Overview

OmniHealth is an Android app for personal medication management, health parameters, medical appointments, and sleep monitoring.  
**No user account is required.** **We do not collect data on our servers.**

---

## 2. Data we process

Data stays **on your device** except where noted below.

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Therapies, medications, intake logs | Reminders and history |
| Vital signs (blood pressure, glucose, SpO₂, weight, temperature, etc.) | Logging and charts |
| Visits, exams, reports (text and photos) | Reminders and archive |
| Medical visit audio | Recorded in «At the visit» with your and your doctor's consent |
| Prescription photos | Local archive |
| Sleep audio | On-device snoring analysis (TensorFlow Lite) |
| Patient profile (name, address, city, phone) | Caregiver/emergency messages (if enabled) |
| Emergency contacts (name, phone) | SMS/calls **you** configure |
| Location | Only if you trigger panic and grant permission; included in SMS to your chosen contacts |

We **do not** read your phone contacts. **No** ads or analytics tracking.

---

## 3. Storage

- Encrypted local database (SQLCipher)
- Audio/photo files in app storage
- Encrypted JSON backup on device; optional copy to `Documents/OmniHealth`

Sleep audio files are **not** included in automatic backup.

---

## 4. Sharing with third parties

**We do not sell your data.**

Data leaves your phone **only if you choose**:

- **SMS** to numbers you enter
- **Calls** to numbers you enter  
- **Location** in panic SMS (if permission granted)

Recipients are telecom carriers and the people whose numbers you configured. **No data is sent to OmniHealth servers.**

---

## 5. Permissions

Microphone, SMS, phone, location, notifications, and alarms are used only for the features above when you enable or use them.

---

## 6. Security

Encrypted database and backup on device. No developer backend transmission.

---

## 7. Deletion

Delete all data via **Android Settings → Apps → OmniHealth → Clear data**, or **uninstall** the app.

---

## 8. Children

The app is for personal health management or supervised caregiver use. Not intended for unsupervised children.
